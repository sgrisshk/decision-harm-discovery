## What C5 computes (fixed here, before any result)

For each trap `T01`-`T05` and each calibration seed 1001-1030 (30 seeds; NOT acceptance seeds, so this is a design-time check and must be repeated on the fresh acceptance seeds in Phase 2c):

* Draws: (a) **v2-clean**: `synthetic_benchmark_v2.generate_v2(V2Config(seed), ())` (no targets, no v1 patterns; covariates identical to v1); (b) **v1**: the real, unmodified v1 generator at the same seed (reference; carries P01-P09). Same analytical builder for both.
* Composite family of a trap: the trap's apparent literal `L` plus one or two engine-language atoms (`_atoms` on the development split, default `DiscoveryConfig()`) on features listed in that trap's `confounded_by` (engine features only; `booking_month` does not exist in the engine language), distinct features, depth <= 3, with the eligibility proxy (development exposure >= 40 and full-cohort exposure <= 40% of rows).
* "Best" is defined by a cheap, pre-validation proxy fixed now: the development-split Welch t statistic of the primary outcome (`contribution_margin_eur`, lower is worse) in the harmful direction, exposed vs unexposed. The top `K = 15` composites per (trap, seed) and the bare literal are graded by the real, unmodified `run_validation` (contract 1.3.0) at multiplicity family **60,000** (ADR-107 D8).
* Reported per trap and draw: fraction of seeds where at least one graded composite reaches `predictive_association` or above; fraction where at least one reaches `policy_readiness` in {`shadow_policy`, `high_confidence`}; the level-2 gate failures of the best composite; the bare-literal rate. The pre-declared bar `tau_t` is NOT chosen here (ADR-107 leaves it to the freeze review); the result is reported as rates with Wilson 95% intervals, and a trap is called "tempting" in this document only if a composite reaches `predictive_association` in >= 50% of seeds.

## Predictions (recorded now; probabilities are the author's)

* **P1.** Bare literals reach `predictive_association` in 0/30 seeds on both draws for every trap (repeats the Phase 1 measurement at family 36,791; family 60,000 is stricter). Confidence 0.9.
* **P2 (v1 draw).** `T03` (`paid_search` + `discount_rate`/`installments`/`customer_type`) and `T04` (`bank_transfer` + `booking_lead_days`/`destination`) composites reach `predictive_association` in >= 50% of seeds (TASK-073/083 history: `paid_search AND discount_rate>=0.03` reached `shadow_policy` on the committed v1 draw; v1's `P03`/`P06` add real signal in those regions). Confidence 0.6.
* **P3 (v2-clean draw).** With no injected patterns, every trap composite is below 50% and at least three of five traps are below 20% (the only signal left is the complexity-mediated confounding, which composites of the confounder features partly capture but which G06 adjustment should also attenuate). Confidence 0.6.
* **P4.** `shadow_policy` or above is reached in <= 10% of seeds by any trap on the v2-clean draw and, on the v1 draw, only by `T03`/`T04` (<= 50%). Under contract 1.3.0 `G16` makes promotion unreachable for k>=2 rules, so `shadow_policy` for composites of k>=2 conditions is expected in 0/30 for every trap on both draws (confidence 0.85 for exactly 0; a non-zero count would be a surprise to be reported and checked against `G16`).
* **P5.** `T01`, `T02`, `T05` composites are the least tempting (each < 30% on both draws). Confidence 0.6.
* **P6 (implication, stated in advance).** If P3 holds, the trap suite `T01`-`T05` on a clean v2 dataset would reject every trap before any confounding gate is relevant (no `predictive_association` to begin with), so "no trap promoted" would again carry little information about G06 and the FC1 blocker would not be satisfiable by reuse alone. If instead composites are reachable, the trap suite tests safety logic to the extent of the measured rate.
